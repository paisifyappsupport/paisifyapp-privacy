FAQ

Common sawaal.

Kya Paisify mera bank account access karta hai? +
Bilkul nahi. Paisify kisi bhi bank account se directly connect nahi karta. Koi bank login, koi account linking, koi screen-scraping nahi. Aap apna bank statement PDF manually download karke import karte ho — wahi PDF jo aapka bank khud deta hai. Aapke banking credentials hamesha sirf aapke paas hi rehte hain.
Kaunse banks ka PDF import supported hai? +
20+ Indian banks — HDFC, ICICI, SBI, Axis, Kotak, PNB, Bank of Baroda, IDFC First, Yes Bank, IndusInd, Canara, Union Bank, Indian Bank, Federal Bank, RBL, AU Small Finance, Utkarsh SFB, IDBI aur aur bhi. Naye banks regularly add hote hain. Agar aapka bank list mein nahi, email karein: paisifyapp.support@gmail.com
Mera financial data kahan store hota hai? +
Aapka saara data aapke phone pe AES-256-GCM encryption ke saath store hota hai — Android Keystore use hota hai. Encryption key kabhi device se bahar nahi jaati. Cloud Backup enable karne pe data encrypted form mein store hota hai, lekin sirf aapka device decrypt kar sakta hai. Paisify ke paas aapke financial data ka access nahi hota kabhi bhi.
SMS detection kaise kaam karta hai bina inbox padhhe? +
Paisify ek Android SMS broadcast receiver use karta hai — yeh sirf incoming SMS sunata hai aur transaction-specific patterns match karta hai. Aapke personal messages, OTPs, aur koi bhi baaki SMS kabhi access nahi hote. Processing poori tarah se locally hoti hai — koi data upload nahi hota. Yeh feature opt-in hai aur aap kabhi bhi band kar sakte ho.
Free vs Pro — kya fark hai? +
Free mein milta hai: 2 PDF imports/month, SMS detection, budget + cashflow, EMI tracker, subscription tracker, savings goals, aur Paisify Score. Pro (₹799/year) unlock karta hai: unlimited imports, AI Coach (Gemini), Deep Financial Report, Month Trends (3 tabs), Net Worth, Tax 80C/80D report, Paisa Wrapped, aur Cloud Backup.
Pro cancel karne ke baad kya hoga mere data ka? +
Aapka saara imported data — transactions, goals, EMIs — aapke phone pe rehta hai. Cancel karne ke baad sirf Pro-exclusive features (AI Coach, Full Report, etc.) lock ho jaate hain. Aapka data kabhi delete nahi hota. Subscriptions Google Play ke through manage hote hain — cancel karna simple hai.
Kya Paisify ek registered financial advisor hai? +
Nahi. Paisify ek personal finance tracking app hai — registered bank, NBFC, ya SEBI-registered investment advisor nahi hai. App ke andar ke saare financial insights informational purposes ke liye hain. Investment, loan, ya tax ke liye kisi qualified Chartered Accountant ya SEBI-registered advisor se milein.
DPDP Act compliance kya hai? Data delete kaise karein? +
Paisify India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 ke anusaar operate karta hai. Humne Shikayat Officer appoint kiya hai as required by law. Data deletion request ke liye email karein: paisifyapp.support@gmail.com — 72 ghante mein process hoga. Privacy policy: paisifyapp.in/privacy
