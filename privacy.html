Who We Are

Paisify ("we", "our", "us") is a personal finance tracking application for Android, published under the package name com.paisify.app. Paisify is not a bank, Non-Banking Financial Company (NBFC), or a SEBI-registered investment advisor. We are a software application that helps you organise and understand your own financial data.

Support & Contact: paisifyapp.support@gmail.com

02 —
What Data We Collect

We collect the minimum data necessary to provide the app's functionality. Here is a precise breakdown:

Data you provide directly
  • Your name and monthly income/budget amount (entered in the app profile)
  • Bank PDF statements you choose to import (processed locally on your device)
  • Transactions you add manually via the Quick Add feature
  • Financial goals (Sapne) you create, including target amounts
  • EMI and subscription details you enter or confirm from PDF import
  • Net worth entries — assets and liabilities you manually add
Data collected automatically
  • Transaction SMS — only if you explicitly grant SMS permission. We receive individual SMS broadcasts matching known Indian bank transaction patterns. Your full SMS inbox is never read. See Section 9.
  • Crash reports (opt-in only) — if you enable this in onboarding or Settings → Privacy, Firebase Crashlytics collects device model, OS version, and stack trace when the app crashes. No financial data is included in crash reports. Disabled by default.
  • Usage analytics (opt-in only) — if you enable this in onboarding or Settings → Privacy, Firebase Analytics records which screens you visit and which features you use. We do not record ₹ amounts, merchant names, or any financial data. Disabled by default.
Payment data
  • Paisify Pro subscriptions are processed entirely through Google Play Billing. We never see, store, or process your payment card details. All billing is managed by Google.
Important: Your bank statement PDF is parsed entirely on your device. The raw PDF is not uploaded to our servers. The extracted transaction data is stored locally in encrypted form on your phone.

05 —
Data Storage & Encryption

All your financial data — transactions, goals, EMIs, subscriptions, profile — is stored exclusively on your device using Android's DataStore, protected by AES-256-GCM encryption.

How the encryption works
  • Local storage encryption keys are generated using the Android Keystore system — a hardware-backed secure enclave on your device
  • The local encryption key is tied to your specific device and never exported or transmitted
  • For locally stored data: Paisify developers cannot decrypt this data because the key never leaves your phone's Keystore.
  • For cloud backup (Pro only, optional): backups are encrypted on your device with a PIN that you choose, before upload. See Section 6 for details. Forgetting the PIN means the backup is permanently unrecoverable — we cannot reset it.
  • If you factory reset your phone or uninstall the app, the local encryption key is destroyed and the data cannot be recovered (unless you have Cloud Backup enabled and remember your PIN)
On-device only: Without Cloud Backup enabled, your financial data never leaves your device. It is not sent to Paisify servers, not stored in the cloud, and not accessible to anyone but you.
06 —
Cloud Backup (Optional)

Paisify Pro includes an optional Cloud Backup feature. This feature is off by default and requires your explicit action to enable.

What gets backed up
  • Your transaction history, goals, EMIs, subscriptions, and profile settings
  • Data is stored in Firebase Firestore (Google's cloud database infrastructure)
  • Data is encrypted on your device with your PIN using PBKDF2-SHA256 (210,000 iterations) + AES-256-GCM before upload. Firestore sees only ciphertext — neither Paisify nor Google can read the contents without your PIN.
  • Access is scoped per-user by Firebase Anonymous Authentication + document-owner rules + Google Play Integrity attestation (App Check)
What does NOT get backed up
  • Your bank PDF files are never uploaded — only the extracted transaction data
  • Your banking credentials are never part of any backup
Cross-device restore

You can restore your backup to a new device using two items: your 64-character device key and your backup PIN. Both are required — knowing the key alone is not enough to decrypt the data. Both the key and the PIN are shown in-app (Settings → Cloud Backup). We cannot recover your PIN. If you lose it, your backup is permanently unrecoverable.

Deleting your backup

You can delete your Cloud Backup at any time from within the app (Aur → Cloud Backup → Delete). You may also request deletion by emailing paisifyapp.support@gmail.com.

07 —
AI Features & Gemini

Paisify Pro includes an AI Money Coach ("Paise") and a Deep Financial Report feature, both powered by Google's Gemini API via Firebase AI Logic.

What data is sent to Gemini

When you use AI features, we send Google's Gemini API a financial summary of your data. This summary does include:

  • Your top 8 merchants by name with their ₹ amounts
  • Spending totals per category (Food, Travel, Shopping, etc.) with ₹ amounts
  • Your monthly income, budget, savings rate, and EMI burden in ₹
  • Names and amounts of your EMIs (e.g., "Bajaj Finance — ₹12,000/month")
  • Names and amounts of your subscriptions (e.g., "Netflix — ₹649/month")
  • Names and ₹ progress of your savings goals
  • Up to 5 recent unusual transactions with merchant name and date

Your data does NOT include bank account numbers, credentials, login details, or raw bank statements.

When data is sent

AI features are opt-in. They are disabled by default and only enabled if you tick the AI Coach box during onboarding or in Settings → Privacy. Even when opted in:

  • On-demand: Each time you tap "Ask AI" or "Generate Report", a financial summary is sent.
  • Automatic (opt-in separately): If you also enable "Monthly Roast" and/or "Weekly Plan", Paisify will send a financial summary once per month or once per week automatically, on app open. Both toggles default off.
  • If you do not enable AI Coach, no data is ever sent to Gemini.
How Gemini handles your data
  • Requests are made via Firebase AI Logic using Anonymous Firebase Authentication — no personal identifier is associated with the API call
  • Google's Gemini API is subject to Google's Privacy Policy and Firebase Terms of Service
  • Google may use prompt content for service operation. We do not train our own AI models on your data.
  • You can use all other Paisify features without ever using AI features
  • You can revoke AI consent at any time in Settings → Privacy. Revocation is immediate.
You are in control: AI features are fully opt-in. Without your consent, Paisify never sends any data to Gemini — not on-demand, not automatically, not in the background.

09 —
SMS & Notifications
SMS Transaction Detection

Paisify can optionally detect bank transaction SMS messages to automatically create transaction entries. This feature:

  • Requires you to explicitly grant RECEIVE_SMS permission
  • Uses an Android SMS BroadcastReceiver that only fires when an SMS arrives — it does not read your inbox history
  • Matches the incoming SMS against known Indian bank transaction patterns (e.g., "debited from your account", "UPI payment of Rs")
  • Processes the match entirely on your device — the SMS content is never uploaded
  • Ignores all non-transaction SMS — personal messages, OTPs, marketing SMS are never accessed
  • Can be disabled at any time in Android Settings → Apps → Paisify → Permissions → SMS
Push Notifications

Paisify may send local notifications for: budget alerts, daily spend reminders, EMI due date reminders, and digest notifications. These are generated on-device and do not require a server connection. You can manage notification preferences in Android Settings → Apps → Paisify → Notifications.

Your Rights Under DPDP Act 2023

As a Data Principal under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the following rights:

Right to Access

You may request a summary of what personal data Paisify holds about you. Email us at paisifyapp.support@gmail.com.

Right to Correction

You can correct your name, income, and budget directly within the app (Aur → Profile → Edit). For other corrections, contact us.

Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten)

You can delete all your data from the app at any time via Aur → Your Data → Clear All Transactions. For complete data deletion including Cloud Backup, email paisifyapp.support@gmail.com. We will process deletion within 72 hours.

Right to Withdraw Consent

You may withdraw consent for SMS permission via Android Settings at any time. You may disable Cloud Backup within the app at any time. You may stop using AI features at any time.

Right to Grievance Redressal

You have the right to file a complaint with our Shikayat Officer (see Section 14) and, if unresolved, with the Data Protection Board of India once it is constituted under the DPDP Act 2023.

To exercise any right: Email paisifyapp.support@gmail.com with subject line "DPDP Data Request — [Your Request Type]". We respond within 72 hours.
