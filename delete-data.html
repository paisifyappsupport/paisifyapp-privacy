Delete Account & Data

Request permanent deletion of your Paisify account and all associated data — in-app or via this form.

Preferred method — in the app

If you have Paisify installed, the fastest way to delete everything is from inside the app:

  • Open Paisify
  • Go to Aur (Settings) → scroll to the bottom
  • Tap Delete Paisify Account
  • Confirm by typing your name

This deletes your local data, your encrypted cloud backup, and your anonymous Firebase identity — everything — in about 5 seconds.

Alternative — request via this form

Use this form if you've uninstalled Paisify or can't access the app. Fill in the details below; we'll process your request within 72 hours per our Privacy Policy and the DPDP Act 2023 §11 Right to Erasure.

⚠️ This cannot be undone. Once deleted, your transaction history, goals, EMIs, subscriptions, and cloud backup are gone forever. We cannot recover any of it.
✅ Request received

We'll email you within 72 hours once your data is deleted. If you don't hear back, reach out to paisifyapp.support@gmail.com.

What gets deleted?

  • All transactions, goals, EMIs, subscriptions, and profile data on your device
  • Your encrypted cloud backup (if any) in Firebase Firestore
  • Your anonymous Firebase Authentication identity
  • Any AI-generated content (monthly roast, weekly plan, financial report) cached on your device

What is NOT deleted?

  • Aggregate, anonymised analytics (if you had opted in) — these are irreversibly de-identified and cannot be tied back to you
  • Crash reports (if you had opted in) — stack traces only, no financial data, retained per Firebase policy for 90 days
  • Google Play purchase records — managed by Google, not us. Cancel your Pro subscription in Play Store.
